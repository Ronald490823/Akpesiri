Career services · Full-stack

MyHireRoad

A career-coaching site with an admin dashboard for CV submissions and built-in notifications.

Read story +

The problem

The founder is a former healthcare COO who now coaches mid-level to C-suite professionals. Those clients are qualified but aren't getting interviews, usually because their resumes list duties instead of results, aren't formatted for applicant tracking systems (ATS), and pair with LinkedIn profiles that tell no clear story. CVs were coming in through email and DMs, so leads were easy to lose and slow to answer.

What I built

On the front, a clear, conversion-focused site lays out the services: resume writing, LinkedIn optimisation, interview coaching, career transition and salary negotiation, plus a booking flow for a consultation. Behind it is a custom backend. Visitors upload their CV, and every submission lands in a secure admin dashboard where the founder can view, download and track each one. A built-in notification system alerts her the moment a new CV arrives, so no lead goes cold.

The outcome

Every CV now lives in one place, with nothing lost in an inbox, and the founder responds faster to the people she's trying to help.

Workspace booking · Nigeria

Flextable

A platform for finding and booking flexible workspaces across Nigerian cities.

Read story +

The problem

Remote workers, freelancers and startup founders in cities like Warri, Asaba, Benin City and Port Harcourt often lose hours looking for a reliable place to work. That means calling around, showing up to full rooms, or settling for a noisy café. Space owners have the opposite problem: empty desks and studios nobody knows exist.

What I built

Flextable is a workspace matchmaker. People can search for and book coworking desks, meeting rooms, and content, photo, audio and film studios. They can also check in, manage bookings, renew subscriptions and leave reviews. Hosts list their own spaces, and the FlexEnabler programme rewards hosts and community members with membership perks for connecting people to spaces.

The outcome

Finding a place to work became a few taps instead of a few phone calls. Flextable now lists spaces in 10 cities and serves 1,000+ users, helping people get work done and helping space owners fill their rooms.
